# Wafiq — Personal Brand Kit
Version 1.0 · Crimson-led editorial identity

## Brand character
Bold, expressive, unconventional, and editorially disciplined. The identity should feel recognisable without becoming loud everywhere. Use strong composition, purposeful negative space, expressive typography, and a controlled use of the accent colours.

## Official five-colour palette

| Colour | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crimson | `#EB0E31` | `235, 14, 49` | Primary signature |\n| Coral | `#FDA2AC` | `253, 162, 172` | Expressive companion |\n| Plum | `#33212C` | `51, 33, 44` | Dark foundation |\n| Sand | `#D8CFC2` | `216, 207, 194` | Warm neutral |\n| Canary Yellow | `#E7C75A` | `231, 199, 90` | Sparing highlight |\n
## Colour roles and combinations
- **Plum + Sand:** default editorial foundation. Use Plum for large dark fields and Sand for readable text or warm light surfaces with Plum text.
- **Crimson + Sand:** signature statement combination. Use Crimson for a hero panel, title block, key link, or call-to-action; Sand for text where contrast is sufficient.
- **Plum + Coral:** expressive but controlled. Prefer Plum text on Coral surfaces; use Coral for large graphic areas, labels, or decorative blocks.
- **Crimson + Coral:** warm, high-energy pairing. Use as adjacent graphic fields or large shapes, not as small text against each other.
- **Canary Yellow + Plum:** high-visibility accent pairing. Use Yellow for small markers, tags, underlines, numbering, or selected highlights with Plum text.
- **Canary Yellow + Crimson:** use sparingly in graphic compositions. Avoid long text in this pairing.

## Suggested starting distribution
Plum 30%, Sand 25%, Crimson 25%, Coral 15%, Canary Yellow 5%. Treat this as a starting point, not a rigid formula. Dark or crimson-led pages are welcome; Sand is a supporting neutral, not a compulsory page background.

## Typography and accessibility rules
- Use Plum or another sufficiently dark text colour on Sand, Coral, and Canary Yellow.
- Use Sand or white text on Plum and Crimson only after checking actual contrast at the intended font size and weight.
- Do not use Coral text on Crimson, or Yellow text on Sand, for small body copy.
- Do not communicate status or meaning through colour alone. Pair colour with labels, icons, or text.
- Test focus indicators, hover states, links, and buttons independently.
- The logo's fine internal cuts may disappear at very small sizes. Use the favicon exports for browser icons and inspect at 16px and 32px.

## Logo handling
The W mark is the signature symbol. Keep its silhouette and proportions intact. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, outline it, or redraw its internal cuts. Use the supplied SVG variants for scalable use. PNG exports are convenience assets. The original artwork is retained in `04_Reference`.

## Important source-file note
The supplied `W.png` is an opaque white-background reference. The supplied `W_transparent.svg` is the vector-traced source used to create the recoloured SVG variants. The original files are preserved unchanged in `04_Reference`. Inspect the recoloured variants against the original before public release, especially at small sizes.
